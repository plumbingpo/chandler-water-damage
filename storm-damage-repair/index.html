How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
